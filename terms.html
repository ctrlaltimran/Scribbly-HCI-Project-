Orders, Pricing & Product Details

In plain English: All prices are clearly listed in Pakistani Rupees (PKR). What you see in your bag is the exact total you pay.

When you place an order with Scribbly Store, we immediately show you a confirmation summary with your order number, items, and total amount. We also email you a full copy of your receipt.

  • Fair Pricing: All items include applicable local taxes. We never add unexpected processing or handling fees at final checkout.
  • Accuracy of Colors: We photograph our notebooks, gel pens, and desk accessories under natural light. Slight color variations may occur depending on your screen settings.
  • Stock Availability: In the rare event that an item sells out before your order is processed, our team will reach out via WhatsApp or call to offer an instant replacement or refund.
02

Shipping & Delivery in Pakistan

In plain English: We deliver nationwide. Orders over PKR 4,999 ship free. Most deliveries arrive in 3 to 5 business days.

We pack every stationery box with protective bubble wrap and recyclable paper stuffing so your fresh notebook corners and delicate highlighter tips arrive in perfect condition.

  • Delivery Timeline: Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad orders typically arrive within 2–4 business days. Other cities and towns across Pakistan arrive within 3–6 business days.
  • Courier Partners: Deliveries are handled by licensed, trusted courier services (e.g. TCS, Leopard, Call Courier) with doorstep delivery and SMS tracking.
  • Delivery Address: Please ensure your delivery phone number is active so the courier rider can call or text before arriving at your door.
03

Payment Options & Cash on Delivery (COD)

In plain English: You can pay with cash when the courier hands you the parcel, or pay securely online with your card.

We believe in flexible, honest payment options tailored for students and families across Pakistan:

  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Pay cash directly to the courier delivery agent. There is no surcharge or extra penalty for choosing COD.
  • Debit / Credit Cards: We accept Visa and Mastercard. All transactions are processed through encrypted, PCI-compliant payment gateways. Scribbly never sees or stores your 16-digit card number or security codes.
  • Bank Transfers & Wallets: Available upon request for large school orders or institutional bulk purchases.

The Happy Desks Guarantee & Returns

In plain English: If an item arrives damaged, flawed, or wrong, we will replace it or refund you within 7 days. No stressful back-and-forth.

We want you to love your Scribbly stationery. If something isn't right, our Happy Desks policy has you covered:

  • Damaged or Incorrect Items: If any pen leaks, notebook page is torn, or you receive the wrong variant, take a quick photo and send it to our WhatsApp support within 7 days. We will dispatch a free replacement immediately.
  • Change of Mind: Unused items in their original, unopened packaging can be returned within 7 days for store credit or exchange.
  • School Orders: Special bulk custom print orders can be adjusted prior to printing commencement.

Privacy Notice & Your Personal Data

In plain English: We collect only what we need to get your order to your desk. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to anyone. Ever.

Your privacy is respected at every step of your Scribbly experience:

  • What We Collect: Your name, phone number, delivery address, and email when you place an order or create an account.
  • How We Use It: Exclusively to fulfill orders, send shipping updates, and respond to your customer support inquiries.
  • No Spam Promise: We only send our newsletter if you actively typed your email into "Join the list". Every newsletter has a clear, one-click unsubscribe button.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Your information is only shared with our courier partner for the sole purpose of physical package delivery. We never share your details with marketing data brokers.
